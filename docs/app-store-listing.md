**Description:**

Plume is a quiet place to breathe.

Choose from fifteen breathing routines drawn from published breathwork sources, organised by what you need: calm, focus, energy, sleep, recovery, or practice. Filter the library with a tap, set your duration, and follow the orb.

The orb is the whole interface during a session. It swells and settles with your breath, with haptics and softly synthesized audio matched to each phase. No timers to read, no screen to study — you can close your eyes.

Made to be lived with:
- Five editorial colour themes, each contrast-tuned for readability
- Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion support throughout
- Clear guidance and contraindications for stronger techniques
- Works entirely offline

And nothing else: no account, no subscription, no analytics, no tracking. Your preferences stay on your phone. Plume collects no data at all.

Breathwork can be intense. Plume explains each technique's intensity, asks you to read the safety guidance once before your first session, and is not a substitute for medical advice.
