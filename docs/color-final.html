Plume · Colour system

Three creams, four ways out.

The shipped schemes were too alike — three warm creams with warm-brown accents, one theme tinted three ways. Four colourists, each with a different craft, re-solved it. The Editor keeps the editorial-paper soul but makes every stock nameable on its own. The Art Director opens a range of light↔dark and warm↔cool, with schemes for breathing after dark. The Dyer sources colour from real materials — indigo vats, cochineal, celadon glaze. The Colour Scientist constructs hues at mathematically maximal separation, verified to stay distinct for colour-blind eyes. Every one of the fourteen clears the floors: ink ≥ 10:1, muted ≥ 4.5:1, accent ≥ 3:1. The orbs are breathing live.
