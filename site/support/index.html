What to Include

When reporting an issue, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Plume app version, the routine you were using, and what happened before the problem appeared.

Safety

Plume offers breathing exercises for general wellbeing, not medical advice. Never force a breath or a hold, and stop if you feel faint, breathless, or distressed.

Use intense routines only while seated or lying down. Never practice rapid breathing or breath holds in water, while driving, or while standing unsupported.

Privacy
