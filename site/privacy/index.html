Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Plume collects no app data.

Plume is designed to run on your iPhone without accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising, or network transmission from the app.

Data Collection

Plume does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data from the app.

Plume does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or third-party data collection tools.

Local App Settings

Plume stores app preferences on your device, such as selected theme, audio cue, haptic settings, last routine, duration, and safety acknowledgements. These settings stay on your device and are not sent to Turf Terrace Ltd or any third party.

Device Permissions

Plume does not access HealthKit, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, notifications, or calendar data.

Contacting Support

If you email support, your email address and message will be used only to respond to your request. Support email is handled outside the Plume app and is not linked to any in-app account because Plume does not create accounts.

Changes

If Plume's privacy behavior changes, this policy will be updated before that change ships.
